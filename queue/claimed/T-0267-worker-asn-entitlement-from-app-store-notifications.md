The JWS header alg must be exactly "ES256" (none, HS256, ES384, absent -> 400), the signature exactly 64 raw bytes verified with the leaf's P-256 key over `header.payload`. X.509 is parsed by an in-repo DER reader (src/der.ts) - no new dependency.
