## Brief

Measured on main at 2026-09-25T19:08:12Z: `find apps/ios -name "*.xcassets" -o -name "AppIcon*"` returns NOTHING and
`grep -c "AppIcon\|ASSETCATALOG" apps/ios/ScenicDrive.xcodeproj/project.pbxproj` is 0. The app installs with the
blank white placeholder icon under the name "Scenic Drive" (Info.plist CFBundleDisplayName). Every other surface of
the walking skeleton has been argued over for weeks - the credit line, the disclaimer, the timing sentence - while
the first thing anyone sees on a home screen is nothing at all.

The plan's token table is the palette (`bg #FFF7ED`, `primary #EA580C`, `scenic #15803D`, `route #2563EB`,
`fg #0F172A`); the product is a scenic drive in Los Angeles, so the drawing is a road and hills, not a compass or a
pin. Keep it flat, legible at 40 pt, and generated rather than hand-placed so it can be re-derived.

`project.pbxproj` is serial and CLAUDE.md says it changes about ten times ever - this is one of those times, and the
diff is two lines.
