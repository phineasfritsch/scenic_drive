  R1 SHAPE. `ScenicKit/RoadTrip/` holds five files, one type each, Foundation only: `RoadTripEdge` (start, end Coordinate, seconds Int, meters Int), `RoadTripPlace` (name, nested `Kind` {stop, lodging}, score Int, coordinate), `RoadTripLimits` (days, maxDriveSeconds, maxMeters), `RoadTripDay` (day, startVertex, endVertex, seconds, meters, stops [String], nested `Overnight` {lodging(name, meters), noLodging}, overnight Overnight? - nil on the last day, which arrives at B) and `RoadTrip` (the engine namespace, nested `Outcome` {plan([RoadTripDay]), overBudget(routeSeconds, ceilingSeconds), tooFewDays(days, reachedVertex)}). Nested types follow RoutePath's DetailRun/Value precedent. Seconds and metres are Int so the whole plan is asserted by exact equality with no float tolerance; "per-day minutes and km" are asserted at that finer grain. Miles reach the engine as metres (the caller converts at 1609.344); departsAt per day and the per-day /plan request are the caller's (the /trip task's) - this module sends nothing, so the one-coordinate, 2-dp server rule is untouched here.
  R2 INPUTS. A route is its edges in order (vertex i = edges[i].start, vertex E = edges[E-1].end) plus a list of candidate places; the engine never fetches. The fastest A->B duration is an input.
  R3 BUDGET +40%, a CEILING. `RoadTrip.budgetSeconds(fastestSeconds:)` = fastest * 40 / 100, integer division (rounds DOWN, the ceiling's safe side); a route whose total seconds exceed fastest + budget returns `.overBudget` and no days. Never a plan over the ceiling.
  R4 FORWARD PASS. Day d of N (1-based) adds the next edge while (a) the day already holds an edge and cumulative seconds * N >= d * total seconds - the day has reached its even share of the trip - is FALSE, and (b) day seconds + edge seconds <= maxDriveSeconds and day metres + edge metres <= maxMeters (both INCLUSIVE). Every day drives at least one edge; a day that cannot take its first edge stops the pass. Boundaries fall on vertices (edges are atomic). Edges left after N days (or a day that took none) -> `.tooFewDays(days: N, reachedVertex:)`, honest, no partial plan. A trip that fits in fewer days than N returns the days it used and never invents an empty day. N < 1 -> tooFewDays at vertex 0; no edges -> plan([]).
  R5 STOPS 2-4. A stop belongs to the corridor when its nearest vertex (haversine, `Geo.distanceMeters`; ties -> lower index) lies within 5_000 m. Day k owns vertices (startVertex, endVertex], and day 1 also vertex 0 - a boundary vertex's stop goes to the day that ENDS there. Each day keeps the top 4 of its corridor stops by score (ties -> lower vertex, then name) and lists them in route order (vertex, then name). A day whose corridor offers >= 2 has 2-4; one that offers 0 or 1 lists what it offers - the minimum is the corridor's, not invented. The 5 km radius is ruled (the plan names no number): on recorded GraphHopper geometry vertices are metres apart, so nearest-vertex distance is the corridor distance; the synthetic fixture's vertices are towns, and its stops sit near them.
  R6 OVERNIGHT. For every day but the last: the nearest lodging to its end vertex with distance <= 15_000 m (ties -> name) -> `.lodging(name, meters: rounded)`; none -> `.noLodging`, honest, the boundary is not moved (backtracking to a town is a later task's ruling, not this engine's).
