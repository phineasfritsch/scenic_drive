## Brief

OWNER DECISION (2026-11-24, in session): "score trunk same". The CLAUDE.md invariant "Motorway/trunk ways carry
scenic_score = 0" becomes "Motorway ways (motorway, motorway_link) carry scenic_score = 0". Trunk and trunk_link
are scored by the formula like any other class. Nothing becomes gated; motorway stays penalised, not excluded.

WHY (measured 2026-11-24, 120 LA trips through services/api's planScenic against the served graph: 18% plan at
+20 min, 38% at +45): PCH is OSM highway=trunk, so Palisades Beach Rd (7.5 km) and Malibu PCH (300/347 sampled
points) score 0 and no coastal plan can pass the honest-failure bar. US mappers use trunk for regionally
important highways, many of them two-lane and scenic; the tag says network importance, not freeway-ness.

WHAT CHANGES
- services/etl: byways.SCENIC_ZERO_CLASSES = {motorway, motorway_link}. Everything importing it follows: score.py
  (the zero rule), normalise (trunk joins the rank population), proximity (the x0.7 "within 150 m of a motorway"
  measures to motorways only, so PCH is not penalised for being near itself), scenecheck, way_record, assemble
  counts, the byway bonus (trunk byways now earn it). regionbuild/osm.MOTORWAY_FILTER follows.
- ScenicKit: SegmentScore.dullClasses follows; RouteScore's dud bucket follows it.
- Fixtures regenerated by their own generators (Tests/Fixtures/scoring/generate.py,
  services/etl/tests/fixtures/generate_way_records.py via plan_oracle.py); gate_parity.json trunk rows stay
  "not gated" and stop being "score 0".
- Guards: the mutation populations, named tests and PINS.yaml statements that quote the four-class set.
- Graph: the LA region rebuilt from the T-0242 store (docs onward: proximity is a pass-1 input), re-imported
  and served.

NOT HERE: any change to the safety gates, the budget ceiling or the motorway rule.
